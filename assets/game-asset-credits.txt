# Asset credits

Licensed meshes and textures were scaled, optimized and converted for Unity/Quest. Tree foliage preserves complete source texture charts; ground contacts and distant cards were adapted. Named shrubs use scans of Corylus avellana and Sambucus nigra leaves on licensed woody branches. Wildlife retains downloaded meshes and textures; lightweight locomotion and resting rigs were added for this game. JUST's gloves were split and adapted for controller grip.

## Free commercial use

Version 0.5 replaces the former noncommercial fox and deer and the personal-use animated sparrow with CC BY models. The active sources below are free to obtain and permit commercial use under their stated terms. CC BY assets require attribution. The cat uses Sketchfab Free Standard; other active assets use CC BY, CC0 or public domain dedications. The adapted dog recording remains CC BY-SA 3.0. Do not use retired source files or old 0.4 builds for commercial distribution. See Builds/license-audit.json for the metadata audit.

JUST's licensed glove mesh was also reshaped into four static basket contact poses (left/right, large/small); its source UVs and texture remain. New mushroom scans were isolated from soil or bark, oriented, rescaled, simplified into LODs and given individual grips. Source author identifications are retained; uncertain specimens are not accepted as recipe ingredients.

Multiplayer avatar 12: Girl with clothes. Worker set by cattleya (CC BY 4.0). Original clothing, skin, hair, skeleton and textures retained; meshes joined, size adapted, mobile materials and controller-driven posing added. Model 15 is excluded because commercial rights to its Wolf3D components are unverified.

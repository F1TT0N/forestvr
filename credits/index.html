“Hymn To The Dawn” by Scott Buckley, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Used as an edited excerpt in the trailer with volume adjustments and fades.

Website imagery

The hero cover and social preview are custom promotional 2D illustrations made with imagegen. They are artwork, not gameplay screenshots. The gallery contains actual game frames: gathering and cooking come from the developer's Meta Quest recordings; the landscape scenes come from the game running on PC. The male/female co-op scene is a staged in-engine demonstration using the game's avatars and animations, not a recording of two connected players. Images are resized and compressed for web delivery.

The trailer uses the approved 67.5-second edit. Development footage may differ from the final release.

ForestVR — promotional film: VR gameplay and co-op feature revision

Gathering, basket pouring, cooking and the successful meal are the user's own Meta Quest recordings supplied on 2026-10-16. Gathering is trimmed by 3 seconds at the start and 2 at the end. Cooking is trimmed by 1 second at the start and 2 at the end. Both remaining intervals are continuous and run at normal speed; only frame-rate conformance and boundary dissolves are applied. No cooking wait has been removed. Original audio is preserved in the separately trimmed clips; the trailer uses the continuous licensed music soundtrack.

The male and female co-op feature scene is staged in-engine on PC with the game's approved multiplayer avatar models (Blender Studio Snow and Female worker) and standing reach/pull/deposit animation. It illustrates the multiplayer feature; it is not a recording of a live connected network session. Cat/dog following uses the current game AI and updated motion. Scenery is existing PC in-game footage. No external pre-rendered scenery, AI models or replacement renders are used.

The earlier trailers and source recordings are preserved. ForestVR development footage, versions 0.10.66–0.10.67.
